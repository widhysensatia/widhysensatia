Project Phoenix

A six-month Digital Capability Transition Program to build internal capability to understand, control, operate, maintain, support, develop and recover Sensatia's digital systems while reducing dependency on any one individual.

Sep 2026 → Feb 2027 StockWiz + Website + MongoDB Knowledge Transfer Evidence-Based Acceptance

Current Phase

September: discovery, inventory, ownership, access and documentation baseline.

Transition in Progress

Core Principle

If critical knowledge is not documented, it should not be considered transferred.

Completion Rule

The program is complete only when capability is demonstrated and acceptance is recorded — not simply because six months have passed.

6-Month Roadmap

From baseline to independent operation

Each month has a practical outcome and evidence expectation. Progress is not inferred from calendar dates alone.

SEPMonth 1

Discovery & Baseline

Understand the complete relevant digital environment and establish the transition baseline.

  • Inventory StockWiz, website, MongoDB, integrations, repositories, environments and third-party services.
  • Record ownership, criticality, access/MFA and recovery arrangements.
  • Capture existing projects, recurring issues, technical debt and risks.
OCTMonth 2

Paired Operation

Widi performs routine work while Colin mentors and reviews.

  • Complete a representative routine change or bug fix.
  • Capture review and testing evidence.
  • Draft deployment, rollback, backup, website-support and MongoDB procedures.
NOVMonth 3

End-to-End Practice

Turn documented knowledge into repeatable operational work.

  • Perform a representative deployment with approvals, tests, rollback preparation and post-release checks.
  • Practise troubleshooting, MongoDB administration and backup restoration.
DECMonth 4

Internal Leadership

Widi leads routine planning and execution; Colin mainly reviews and advises.

  • Lead intake, prioritization, estimates, solution design, routine changes and deployments.
  • Validate that documentation is usable by another appropriately skilled internal person.
JANMonth 5

Readiness Rehearsal

Demonstrate routine independence and prepare the final evidence pack.

  • Rehearse StockWiz support, production release, website troubleshooting and recovery.
  • Review critical accounts, MFA and recovery; close or formally document exceptions.
FEBMonth 6

Independent Operation & Validation

Demonstrate internal capability and conduct the formal completion review.

  • Finalize asset/access records, documentation, transfer log, roadmap and final handover report.
  • Record outstanding matters explicitly and accept only when criteria are demonstrated.

Governance Rules

Evidence before assumptions

Project Phoenix is designed to create auditable internal capability, not just a collection of documents.

Unknown stays unknown

If information is not verified, mark it as Unknown / To Verify. Do not turn an assumption into a fact.

Evidence is required

Technical facts, test dates, approvals and capability claims must be backed by real evidence.

Owner + date

Every carried-forward action needs an owner and target date.

No secrets in docs

Store passwords, tokens, API keys and recovery codes only in the approved secure secret store; document only references.

Reuse valid evidence

Existing Confluence, repository docs and Jira work can be reused when they genuinely satisfy the requirement.

Stability first

Production stability takes priority over transition speed.

Known open gaps

Complete production/environment access list, formal deploy/rollback procedures, scheduled jobs/webhooks/services inventory, legacy failure patterns, and internal backup coverage when Widi is unavailable.

Completion

Definition of program complete

February is a target review point, not an automatic pass date.

Internal team can routinely maintain and support StockWiz.
Deployment and rollback can be performed safely using documented procedures.
Routine production issues can be diagnosed internally.
Core integrations and dependencies can be explained and supported.
Backup/recovery is documented and has practice/validation evidence.
Database changes can be managed safely.
Website and MongoDB areas in scope have sufficient internal capability.
Critical architecture, database structure, integrations and recovery are documented.
Critical accounts, MFA and recovery paths are under company control.
No critical operational knowledge exists only with Colin.
Final handover evidence and transition acceptance are recorded.
