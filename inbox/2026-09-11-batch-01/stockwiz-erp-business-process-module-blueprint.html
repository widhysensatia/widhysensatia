Ringkasan eksekutif

Blueprint ini menjawab “ERP apa yang perlu dibangun?” sebelum tim memutuskan tabel, API, atau layar.

Arah desain bisnis

Satu aliran bisnis, satu sumber kebenaran per domain, dan jejak lengkap dari bahan baku hingga penjualan.

StockWiz Next bukan kumpulan menu departemen. Ia harus menghubungkan permintaan, pembelian, penerimaan, QC, produksi, gudang, penjualan, keuangan, customer, dan tenaga kerja melalui dokumen serta event yang konsisten.

02

Prinsip desain ERP

Prinsip ini menjadi pagar saat kebutuhan baru muncul dan mencegah setiap departemen membangun sumber datanya sendiri.

01 · Ownership

Satu system of record

Setiap entitas dan saldo hanya memiliki satu pemilik. Modul lain membaca melalui contract atau read model.

02 · Transaction

Dokumen menggerakkan ledger

Stok, uang, dan status tidak diubah manual tanpa source document, actor, reason, dan approval.

03 · Traceability

Lot-first manufacturing

Lot, expiry, status kualitas, formula version, material genealogy, dan custody adalah bagian inti desain.

04 · Control

Maker-checker

Pembuat, approver, receiver, QC releaser, payer, dan reconciler dipisahkan sesuai nilai serta risiko.

05 · Evolution

Modular dan bertahap

Setiap vertical slice memberi nilai dan dapat digunakan tanpa menunggu seluruh ERP selesai.

06 · Evidence

Audit dan KPI by design

Setiap perubahan penting menghasilkan audit event dan metrik proses, bukan hanya log teknis.

Model organisasi & fasilitas

Scope data, approval, costing, dan laporan mengikuti struktur perusahaan. Struktur ini wajib disepakati sebelum role dan transaksi dibuat.

Governance

Legal Entity

Company, currency, fiscal calendar, tax registration, chart of accounts, dan statutory reporting.

→
Operations

Plant & Site

Pabrik, kantor, retail/shop, distribution center, lab, dan channel digital dengan timezone serta operating calendar.

→
Execution

Warehouse & Location

Zone, bin, staging, receiving, quarantine, sampling, production, finished goods, damaged, dan dispatch.

Financial dimension

Cost center & profit center

Departemen, plant, channel, brand, dan project untuk budget, actual cost, margin, serta accountability.

Manufacturing dimension

Work center & resource

Mixing vessel, filling line, packaging line, lab equipment, capacity, shift, calendar, dan maintenance status.

Commercial dimension

Sales organization & channel

Retail, e-commerce, wholesale, distributor, partnership, territory, price list, customer segment, dan salesperson.

Scope rule: user tidak otomatis melihat semua data karena memiliki role. Akses selalu merupakan kombinasi permission + legal entity + plant/site + warehouse/shop + department + approval limit.

Procure-to-Pay

Menghubungkan kebutuhan bahan dengan supplier, penerimaan, QC, hutang, dan pembayaran tanpa kehilangan tiga-way match.

Alur pembelian bahan dan jasa

Dimulai dari kebutuhan yang disetujui dan berakhir pada liability serta payment yang direkonsiliasi.

Owner: Procurement + Finance
01Purchase requisitionItem/service, quantity, need date, cost center, budget.
02RFQ & comparisonSupplier quotes, price, lead time, MOQ, terms, quality.
03Purchase orderApproved commitment, version, schedule, tax, currency.
04ASN & receivingDelivery, GRN, quantity, supplier lot, damage, documents.
05Quarantine & QCSample, inspect, release/reject, COA verification.
06Supplier invoicePO–receipt–invoice three-way match dan variance.
07PaymentDue date, approval, bank control, settlement, journal.
Dokumen/status
  • PR: draft → submitted → approved/rejected → sourced
  • PO: draft → approved → sent → partial → received → closed/cancelled
  • GRN: received → quarantine → accepted/rejected
Kontrol & exception
  • Approval limit dan budget check
  • Supplier block/qualification
  • Over/under receipt tolerance
  • Price/quantity/tax variance workflow
  • Perubahan bank supplier dual approval
KPI
  • PR-to-PO cycle time
  • On-time/in-full supplier
  • Purchase price variance
  • QC rejection rate
  • Invoice match exception dan days payable
Integrasi: MRP membuat planned requisition, Procurement mengubahnya menjadi PO, Warehouse membuat GRN, QC menentukan stock status, Inventory mem-post movement, dan Finance membuat accrual/AP—tanpa modul menulis data satu sama lain secara langsung.

Inventory-to-Fulfillment

Inventory menjadi shared operational ledger untuk bahan, bulk, WIP, finished goods, sample, dan spare part pada semua lokasi.

Alur inventory dan warehouse

Semua perpindahan memiliki source document, lot/status, from/to location, quantity/UOM, actor, dan posting time.

Owner: Inventory & Warehouse
01ReceiveGRN/return/production output ke receiving atau quarantine.
02Inspect/statusAvailable, quarantine, rejected, damaged, expired.
03PutawayDirected destination by zone/bin, compatibility, capacity.
04ReserveATP/FEFO allocation untuk sales atau production.
05Pick & moveScan item/lot/bin, staging, transfer, custody.
06Issue/shipConsume ke produksi atau dispatch ke customer/shop.
07Count/reconcileCycle count, variance approval, reversal, audit.
Inventory dimensions
  • Legal entity, site, warehouse, zone/bin
  • Item, variant, lot, serial bila perlu
  • Status, ownership, reservation, in-transit
  • Base UOM dan conversion snapshot
Aturan wajib
  • Available tidak negatif tanpa policy
  • FEFO untuk expiry-controlled item
  • Posted movement immutable
  • Transfer out = transit + receipt + variance
  • Ledger = balance projection
KPI
  • Inventory accuracy dan variance age
  • Stockout/overstock
  • Expired/near-expiry value
  • Pick accuracy dan order cycle time
  • Dock-to-stock dan space utilization
Core document

Inventory movement

Append-only ledger dengan unique source key, movement type, lot/status, cost reference, dan reversal link.

Fast read

Balance & reservation

Projection per dimension, di-update dalam transaction yang sama dan dapat dibangun ulang dari ledger.

Control

Reconciliation case

Variance otomatis menjadi exception case dengan severity, evidence, owner, SLA, resolution, dan approval.

Alur produksi batch

Hanya material berstatus released, lot valid, dan formula efektif yang boleh digunakan.

Owner: Production + QA
01Production orderItem, batch size, formula/routing version, due date.
02Material stagingFEFO allocation, pick list, issue reservation.
03WeighingScan material/lot, target/tolerance, scale/operator.

05Bulk outputActual yield, bulk lot, sample, quarantine, loss.
06Fill & packPackaging issue, line clearance, FG lot, reconciliation.
07Close orderVariance, scrap/rework, labor/machine, cost, approval.
Electronic batch record

  • Operator, equipment, timestamp, parameter
  • Material lot genealogy dan actual quantity
  • Deviation, hold, e-signature, attachment
Kontrol proses
  • Line clearance dan equipment status
  • Weighing tolerance + second verification
  • No expired/quarantine material
  • Yield/loss tolerance dan variance reason

KPI
  • Schedule adherence dan cycle time
  • Yield, material variance, scrap/rework
  • Right-first-time batch
  • OEE/downtime bila data cukup
  • Cost per batch/unit
Genealogy minimum: setiap finished-goods lot harus dapat ditelusuri ke bulk lot, semua raw/packaging lots, formula/specification version, production order, work center, operator, QC results, dan shipment/customer yang menerimanya.

Quality, batch release & recall

QC melakukan pengujian; QA memegang disposition dan sistem mutu. Status kualitas langsung mengendalikan apakah stok boleh dipakai atau dijual.

Quality lifecycle

Dari inspection lot sampai release, deviation, CAPA, complaint, dan recall.

Owner: QA/QC
01Inspection lotDibuat dari receipt, production stage/output, atau complaint.
02SampleSampling plan, chain of custody, sample label, retention.
03Test resultParameter, method, instrument, analyst, result, attachment.
04ReviewSpecification comparison, OOS/OOT, second review.
05DispositionRelease, reject, conditional, rework, return, destroy.
06CAPAInvestigation, root cause, action, effectiveness check.
Quality records
  • Specification dan test method version
  • COA incoming/finished goods
  • Deviation, OOS/OOT, non-conformance
  • CAPA, change control, training evidence
Release control
  • QC result tidak sama dengan QA disposition
  • Released status hanya oleh authorized QA
  • Result correction memiliki reason/audit
  • Expired calibration dapat memblokir result
KPI
  • First-pass release dan release lead time
  • Supplier/production defect rate
  • OOS/OOT dan repeat test
  • CAPA overdue/effectiveness
  • Complaint rate by product/lot

Batch-to-Recall

Recall dapat melakukan trace backward dan forward dalam hitungan menit, bukan pencarian manual lintas collection.

Owner: QA + Legal/Commercial
01SignalComplaint, test, authority, supplier, trend.
02AssessSeverity, scope, risk, decision authority.
03HoldBlock affected lot/location/order immediately.
04Trace backwardFG → bulk → raw/packaging lot → supplier.
05Trace forwardLot → transfer → shipment → shop/customer.
06ExecuteNotify, return, segregate, reconcile, destroy/rework.
07Close & CAPAEffectiveness, quantity reconciliation, authority report.

Record-to-Report, finance & costing

Finance menerima subledger event dari procurement, inventory, manufacturing, sales, payment, payroll, dan assets; bukan menghitung ulang transaksi dari laporan manual.

Record-to-Report

Setiap journal memiliki source, accounting date, dimension, rule version, dan reconciliation status.

Owner: Finance
01Subledger eventReceipt, invoice, issue, output, shipment, payment, payroll.
02Accounting ruleAccount mapping, dimension, currency, tax, valuation.
03JournalBalanced debit/credit, source reference, posting period.
04ReconcileAP/AR/bank/inventory/COGS/payroll vs GL.
05CloseCutoff, accrual, valuation, adjustment, lock period.
06ReportP&L, balance sheet, cash flow, margin, audit pack.
Core capabilities
  • Chart of accounts dan financial dimensions
  • GL, AP, AR, bank/cash, tax, fixed assets
  • Budget/commitment dan expense
  • Multi-currency dan exchange difference
Inventory & production cost
  • Valuation method per policy/entity
  • Standard/actual material, labor, overhead
  • Purchase price/material/yield variance
  • COGS saat fulfillment dan return reversal
Close controls & KPI
  • Subledger-to-GL difference = 0
  • Close checklist/owner/evidence
  • Days to close, DSO, DPO, cash forecast
  • Margin by SKU/channel/customer
Boundary: desain accounting rule, tax, valuation, currency rounding, dan period close wajib disetujui Finance serta advisor pajak/audit yang relevan. ERP tidak boleh “membetulkan” uang dengan floating-point atau jurnal tanpa source.

Kontrol lintas modul & segregation of duties

Kontrol platform menghindari setiap modul membuat mekanisme approval, audit, numbering, file, dan permission sendiri.

Identity

RBAC + scope + limit

Permission action ditambah organization/site/warehouse/department scope dan amount/quantity approval limit.

Workflow

Approval engine

Rule berversi, maker-checker, delegation ber-expiry, escalation, SLA, reason, dan evidence.

Audit

Immutable audit event

Actor, time, action, entity, before/after ter-redact, reason, source, correlation, device/IP context.

Reliability

Idempotency & outbox

Unique operation key, transaction boundary, durable event, retry/backoff, dedup, dead-letter, replay.

Integration

Contract & inbox

Versioned API/event, signed webhook, incoming dedup, mapping, reconciliation, rate budget, circuit breaker.

Documents

File & e-signature

Private object storage, scan, retention, version, checksum, authorized download, signature evidence.

Konflik tugas yang harus dicegah

Procurement

Membuat supplier/PO dan menyetujui PO atau mengubah rekening serta membayar.

Maker ≠ approver ≠ payer
Warehouse

Membuat transfer, menerima transfer sendiri, lalu menyetujui variance.

Sender ≠ receiver ≠ reconciler
Quality

Melakukan test, mengubah result, dan merilis lot tanpa reviewer.

Analyst ≠ QA disposition
Manufacturing

Mengeluarkan material, melaporkan output, dan menyetujui yield variance.

Operator ≠ variance approver
Sales

Mengubah price/discount, membuat order, dan menyetujui refund sendiri.

Seller ≠ exception approver
Finance

Membuat vendor, invoice, payment, dan bank reconciliation.

Master ≠ posting ≠ payment ≠ reconcile
HR

Mengubah role/employee sendiri atau menyetujui leave/overtime miliknya.

Self-service ≠ approval
Platform

Developer production mengubah data dan menghapus audit/log tanpa kontrol.

Build ≠ deploy ≠ production data repair

Roadmap modul & keputusan manajemen

Wave mengikuti dependency bisnis. Timeline adalah rentang perencanaan dan dikalibrasi setelah discovery, kapasitas tim, serta kesiapan data.

Pra-wave · 0–3 bulan

Stabilkan sistem existing

Tutup P0, lindungi stock transaction, pasang test/CI/observability, profile data, dan tetapkan domain owner.

  • Exit: security P0 tertutup, transaksi kritis idempotent/atomic, variance terlihat.
Wave 1 · 3–8 bulan

Foundation & inventory pilot

Organization, item/UOM, identity/RBAC, workflow/audit, PostgreSQL foundation, inventory ledger, lot/status, transfer pilot.

  • Exit: satu warehouse flow live, ledger=balance, source-of-truth ownership jelas.
Wave 2 · 6–15 bulan

Procurement, planning, manufacturing & quality

PR/PO/GRN, supplier quality, formula/BOM/routing, MRP, production order/batch record, QC release, genealogy, recall.

  • Exit: bahan baku → finished lot sepenuhnya traceable dan inventory status dikendalikan QA.
Wave 3 · 12–24 bulan

Commercial & finance

Party/customer, unified order/fulfillment/returns, pricing/promotion/loyalty, AP/AR, payment, valuation, costing, GL, close.

  • Exit: order-to-cash dan procure-to-pay reconcile; subledger cocok dengan GL.
Wave 4 · 18–30 bulan

People, assets, analytics & decommission

HR/workforce, competency, maintenance/calibration, governed analytics; legacy route/job/collection dipensiunkan.

  • Exit: legacy read-only/archive, control dan KPI lintas domain beroperasi.

Keputusan yang harus ditandatangani

Decision 01

Inventory ownership

Siapa pemilik balance, movement, reservation, lot, dan adjustment lintas channel?

Decision 02

Organization model

Legal entity, plant, warehouse, shop, channel, cost/profit center, timezone.

Decision 03

Quality authority

Pemisahan QC result, QA disposition, deviation, rework, destruction, dan recall.

Decision 04

Formula governance

Owner formula/BOM/spec, version approval, effective date, alternate, dan change control.

Decision 05

Financial policies

Currency/rounding, valuation, costing, tax, accounting date, dimensions, dan closing.

Decision 06

Commercial model

Satu order/customer lifecycle dengan channel metadata, price/promo, return, dan loyalty.

Decision 07

Privacy & retention

Data classification, consent, PII scope, archive, legal hold, deletion, dan historical access.

Decision 08

Build vs integrate

Fungsi mana inti StockWiz dan mana yang lebih tepat memakai layanan khusus melalui integration contract?

Decision 09

Accountability

Sponsor, product owner, domain owners, data owner, tech lead, control owner, dan KPI owner.

Langkah berikutnya

Jalankan workshop per value stream, validasi proses normal dan exception, tetapkan owner serta invariant, lalu pilih warehouse transfer sebagai vertical slice pertama. Blueprint ini menjadi business north star; implementation blueprint Opsi 3 menjelaskan cara memindahkan ownership modul secara bertahap.
