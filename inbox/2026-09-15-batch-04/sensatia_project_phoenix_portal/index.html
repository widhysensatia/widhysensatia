Program overview

Apa yang harus dicapai Phoenix

Scope resmi lebih luas dari StockWizz-only: sistem, akses, infrastructure knowledge, operating practices, website, MongoDB, integrations dan recovery.

Understand & control

Asset inventory, ownership, repositories, environment, access, MFA, third-party and dependencies harus diketahui perusahaan.

Operate & maintain

Routine support, development, deployment, troubleshooting dan data/integration operation harus dapat dijalankan internal.

Recover & transfer

Backup/recovery harus terdokumentasi dan diuji secara proporsional; critical knowledge tidak boleh hanya berada pada satu individu.

Roles

Governance responsibilities

Widi

DCTP Technology Capability Lead (program role). Coordinate records, DTAR, requests, evidence and progressively lead routine development/support/deployment.

Colin

Technical Advisor / Mentor. Transfer historical/system knowledge, review work, validate evidence and progressively reduce routine implementation.

Agus

Infrastructure contribution. Supply/validate hardware, access, infrastructure and recovery information within his responsibilities; peer role with Widi.

Michael

Executive Sponsor. Confirm priorities, resolve escalations, review readiness and make the final transition acceptance decision.
