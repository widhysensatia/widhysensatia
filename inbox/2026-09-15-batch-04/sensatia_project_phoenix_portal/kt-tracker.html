REG-002 session fields

Session ID, date, topic/Asset IDs, participants, knowledge transferred, Widi performed/demonstrated, Colin guidance required, documentation reference, open questions, next action, owner, due date, status and hours used.

Readiness scale

1 highly dependent · 2 substantial guidance · 3 occasional guidance · 4 independently handles routine work with evidence · 5 independently capable including appropriate diagnosis and recovery.

Evidence pattern

Discussed → documented → Widi demonstrates/performs → validator evidence → remaining dependency/next action. A topic should not jump straight from “discussed” to “transferred”.
