Final Handover Report

Must summarize transition scope/result, systems/Asset IDs, demonstrated internal capability, access/MFA, architecture, integrations, deployment/rollback, recovery evidence, KT, outstanding matters, residual dependencies and roadmap.

Completion Certificate

Acceptance records what is complete and explicitly lists outstanding matters. Optional advisory arrangement remains a separate written decision.
