DG-010 · SENS-160

Backup & Recovery Center

Baseline recovery requirements untuk critical systems. DG-010 menegaskan bahwa backup job yang sukses saja tidak membuktikan recoverability; reasonable restore tests harus dilakukan dan direkam.

RTO/RPO must be agreedRestore evidence requiredProtect production continuity

Current phase

September: discovery, inventory, ownership, access and documentation baseline.

Transition in Progress

Core principle

If critical knowledge is not documented, it should not be considered transferred.

Completion rule

Capability must be demonstrated and acceptance recorded. February is a target review point, not an automatic pass date.

Business requirements

Asset ID, Business Owner, business impact, proposed RTO/RPO and approval reference. Values must be agreed, not assumed.

Backup configuration

Scope, frequency, retention, location, encryption, access, monitoring, primary and alternate responsible person.

Recovery proof

Procedure, restore target, prerequisites, steps, result, evidence, unresolved gaps, next test and acceptance authority.

Do not infer recovery from backup

A “successful backup” is not enough. Recovery must be reasonably demonstrated/tested, with actual result and unresolved gaps recorded.

Continuity question

Phoenix explicitly keeps internal backup coverage visible when Widi is unavailable after transition. Alternate operational ownership is part of continuity, not only data backup.
